## Audio status

Sound on. Full mix: voice, room tone, phone-line hiss, eight footsteps, one grab,
a front door and a latch. No licensed music, nothing to clear.

The last 1.8 s is digital silence on purpose. Do not let a platform normalise or
pad it; the door → latch → silence is the ending.

## Sound-off check

The captions carry the plot without audio: `it's inside` → `count the doors` →
`one of them is open` → `you can't see me` → `i can see you` → `five steps away`
→ `closer`. A muted viewer still gets the point of view flip, because the caller
name on screen is **You**, not **Me**.
