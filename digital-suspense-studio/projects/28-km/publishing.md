# Publishing: 2.8 km

## Suggested title

2.8 km

*Alternate:* Find My shows a second you

## Caption

there are two phones on this map. one of them is yours.

it closes 2.8 km without her taking a step.

how close before you'd stop looking?

(FICTIONAL STORY — invented devices, invented map, invented street.)

## Disclosure

This is a work of fiction. All names, devices, maps and events are invented.
The map is drawn, not real; no real address is shown. No account, device or
number in this film exists.

## Cover

- **Frame:** 200 (0:06.67) — the two pins approaching, chip reading `400 m`, before
  the name resolves.
- **Cover text:** `one of these is you` (five words).
- **Why not later:** frame 630 shows the pin reading **ASHA**, which is the twist. The
  cover must not spend it.
- **Check:** the chip and the pin chip both sit inside the middle third, so a 4:5 or
  1:1 crop keeps the readout legible.

## Audio status

Sound on. Full mix: room tone, seven proximity pings rising 1100 → 1430 Hz, one
notification chime at 10.0 s, and one dropout at 24.5 s. No music, no voice-over,
nothing to license.

The last 3.0 s are digital silence on purpose — the empty map is meant to be heard as
nothing at all. Do not let a platform normalise or pad the tail.

**Not yet auditioned.** Levels are measured on the encoded file (see `story.md`); no
human has listened to the mix end to end.

## Sound-off check

Everything the story needs is on screen: the distance falls, the pin is named, the
device row empties, the map goes blank. The captions carry the second layer and never
repeat a spoken line, because there are no spoken lines.

## Asset rights

## Known review issues

- The mix has not been auditioned by ear. Treat the level table in `story.md` as a
  measurement, not a review.
- The map is stylised: no street names, no landmarks, no real place. If a platform
  audience reads it as a real map, the caption's disclosure line covers it.
- The film has no end card by design. The fiction label still renders on every scene,
  including the empty one.
