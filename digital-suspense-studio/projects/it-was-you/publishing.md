## Suggested title

It was you.

## Caption

the phone is on the floor, still counting. someone walks out of your front door.
then the phone is picked up, the call ends, and a message gets recorded.

the last frame is the first frame of part 1. that's the whole ending — watch them
back to back.

## Disclosure

This is a work of fiction. All names, accounts, numbers and events are
invented. The voice is synthetic. This is the last part; post it after parts 1–3
or the loop has nothing to land on.
