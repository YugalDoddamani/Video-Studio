## Audio status

Sound on. This cut has a full mix: voice-over, room tone, chime, static,
ringtone and knocks. It is not a music track — there is no licensed music and
nothing to clear.

The last 1.8 s is digital silence on purpose. Do not let a platform
normalise or pad it; the knock → silence → text is the ending.

## Sound-off check

The caption track carries the whole story without audio, and the voice-over is
also transcribed in the player from 0:03 to 0:21. A muted viewer still gets:
tomorrow's timestamp → your own voice → 3 knocks, 9PM → the cut → the call.
