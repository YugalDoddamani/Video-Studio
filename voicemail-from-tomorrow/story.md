# Story: Voicemail from Tomorrow (Revised)

## Project details
- Topic: A woman receives a voicemail from tomorrow.
- Slug: voicemail-from-tomorrow
- Target duration: 40 seconds
- Fiction disclosure: Fictional digital suspense.

## Characters
- Sarah: Early 30s, tired eyes, wearing an oversized grey hoodie.
- Future Sarah: Same as Sarah, but sounds breathless and terrified.

## Story structure
- **Hook**: Close-up of Sarah's face in the dark, lit only by her phone. A notification pops up: "Voicemail from: Me".
- **Setup**: She plays it. The screen shows the voicemail player. We hear her own voice from tomorrow, screaming about a "blue truck" and "the bridge".
- **Escalation**: Sarah looks toward her window. Cut to a static shot of a mysterious blue truck idling in the rain outside her house.
- **Twist**: The voicemail ends with a crash sound. The phone screen glitches. The time on the phone jumps forward 24 hours instantly.
- **Ending**: Sarah looks at her reflection in the phone screen. Behind her in the reflection, the door to her room slowly opens.

## Scene breakdown

### Scene 01: The Hook
- Type: Character/Phone
- Duration: 150 frames (5s)
- Visual: Split screen. Left: Sarah's face (static, anxious). Right: Phone screen showing "New Voicemail from Me".
- Motion: Phone screen glows, pulsing light on Sarah's face.

### Scene 02: The Warning
- Type: Phone UI
- Duration: 450 frames (15s)
- Visual: Fullscreen voicemail player. Waveform moving.
- Motion: Text transcript appearing word-by-word as the audio plays.

### Scene 03: The Blue Truck
- Type: Environment
- Duration: 300 frames (10s)
- Visual: Sarah's POV looking through rainy glass. A dark blue truck is parked in the street.
- Motion: Rain streaks on glass, truck headlights flicker.

### Scene 04: The Glitch & The Door
- Type: Character/Reflection
- Duration: 300 frames (10s)
- Visual: Close-up of Sarah's face reflected in the black phone screen. A dark silhouette appears in the doorway behind her.
- Motion: Subtle zoom into the reflection; the silhouette moves closer.
